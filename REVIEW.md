# Fitness review checklist

Going through the training rules one at a time before adding new features. For each item: what the app does now, and the question to settle. Decisions get written in as we go. What the recommended fitness sites say, and where they disagree, is in `GUIDELINES.md`.

## Phases

- [ ] **1. Phase ranges.** Now: endurance 15–20 reps, 30–60s rest; hypertrophy 6–12 reps, 3–6 sets, 30–90s rest; strength 1–5 reps, 2–5 min rest; power 1–10 reps, 2–5 min rest. Only hypertrophy has a set range (other phases default to 3 sets). *Should every phase have a set range?*
- [ ] **2. Phase length and order.** Now: 13 weeks each, cycle endurance → hypertrophy → strength → power. Beginners (or anyone with a health flag) wanting strength or power start with hypertrophy as a foundation. *Right order? Same length for every phase?*
- [ ] **3. Deload weeks.** Now: none. Training straight through 13 weeks. *Add a lighter week (e.g. every 4–6 weeks, or at the end of each phase)?*
- [ ] **4. Power phase progression.** Now: power progresses like the others (+1 rep). Power reps are meant to stay fast, so extra reps may work against it. *Progress power by weight only, or keep reps?*

## Tiny steps

- [ ] **5. Step sizes.** Now: +1 rep, +2.5 kg / 5 lb, −5s rest. *Right sizes? Should dumbbells and kettlebells use bigger jumps (gyms often go 2 kg dumbbells, 4 kg kettlebells)?*
- [ ] **6. Step order.** Now: rotates reps → weight → rest. Miss once: same step again. Miss twice: switch to a different kind of step. *Keep this order and the two-miss rule?*
- [ ] **7. Adding sets.** Now: sets never go up by themselves; only reps, weight and rest change. Hypertrophy allows 3–6 sets. *Add "+1 set" as a fourth kind of step?*
- [ ] **8. What counts as a hit.** Now: every set must reach the target reps at the target weight. One short set means a miss. *Too strict? Allow one set to be a rep short?*
- [ ] **9. Rep cap.** Now: at the top of the rep range on every set, the only step is weight, with reps dropping just enough (12 @ 135 → 11 @ 140 lb). *Drop just enough, or reset to the bottom of the range (e.g. 6 reps)?*
- [ ] **10. First session.** Now: the first time with a routine, whatever you log becomes the baseline (lowest reps and lowest weight across sets). *Is that the right starting point, or should it suggest a weight?*
- [ ] **11. Bodyweight exercises.** Now: they progress by reps only, and get stuck at the top of the range (e.g. 5 push-ups in the strength phase). *How should bodyweight moves progress: added weight, harder variations, or a higher rep range?*

## Rest

- [ ] **12. Compound vs isolation split.** Now: compound lifts use the longer half of each phase's rest range, isolation the shorter half (strength: compound 3.5–5 min, isolation 2–3.5 min). *Right split point?*
- [ ] **13. Beginner rest.** Now: beginners never rest under 60s. *Keep?*

## Strength numbers

- [ ] **14. Estimated one-rep max.** Now: Epley formula. It gets less accurate above about 10 reps (endurance is 15–20). *Keep Epley, or blend formulas, or only estimate from sets of 12 or fewer?*
- [ ] **15. Changing phase.** Now: targets convert using estimated max (4 × 11 @ 145 lb becomes 4 × 5 @ 165 lb in strength). *Sensible, or start a little lighter after a phase change?*

## Exercises and plans

- [ ] **16. Exercise tags.** Now: each exercise is tagged compound/isolation, equipment, common, explosive, advanced. *Spot-check the tags for the exercises you use.*
- [ ] **17. Injury lists.** Now: the riskiest moves for each area are switched off (e.g. sore lower back switches off deadlifts and barbell rows). *Too cautious, or not enough?*
- [ ] **18. Weekly splits.** Now: 1–2 days full body; 3 days full body (beginners) or push/pull/legs; 4 upper/lower; 5 push/pull/legs + upper/lower; 6 push/pull/legs twice. *Right splits for each number of days?*
- [ ] **19. Weekly volume.** Now: not tracked. Hypertrophy guidance is often around 10–20 hard sets per muscle per week. *Show sets per muscle group per week, or warn when it's low or high?*
- [ ] **20. Session time estimate.** Now: about 45 seconds per set plus rest, a minute to set up each exercise, and a 5-minute warm-up. *Match your real sessions?*
- [ ] **21. Warm-up sets.** Now: none. *Suggest warm-up sets before heavy compound lifts?*

## Goals and levels

- [ ] **22. Goal targets.** Now: suggested lift improvement for 3 months is +20% (new), +10% (some experience), +5% (experienced). *Realistic?*
- [ ] **23. XP values and level speed.** Now: +5 show up, +10 rep or rest step, +15 weight step, +25 new best. Early levels every 2–3 workouts of an exercise. *Right pace?*
