# Fitness guidelines

What the five recommended sites say, compared with what the app does now. Where they agree, the app follows them. Where they disagree, Dustin decides and the decision is written into `REVIEW.md`.

Sites: NASM, ACE, Healthline, Nerd Fitness, ExRx. **ExRx blocks automated reading**, so it isn't covered here. Paste its text in by hand if you want it included. Nerd Fitness and ACE often quote the NSCA, so "NSCA" below means "as quoted by those sites".

## Where the sites agree

| Topic | What they say | App now |
|---|---|---|
| Hypertrophy reps | 6–12 (all) | 6–12 ✓ |
| Strength reps | 1–5 (ACE, NASM, Nerd Fitness, Healthline) | 1–5 ✓ |
| Load by goal | Endurance ≤67% of max, hypertrophy 67–85%, strength ≥85% (ACE, Nerd Fitness/NSCA, NASM) | Not used directly; reps set the load |
| Beginners | Full body 2–3 days a week; learn form with light weight first; start too light rather than too heavy (all) | 1–3 days full body for beginners ✓ |
| When to add weight | Only after you can beat your rep target with good form (all; details differ, see conflict 9) | Rotates reps → weight → rest; weight forced at top of rep range |
| Recovery | About 48 hours before training the same muscles hard again (ACE, Nerd Fitness, Healthline) | Splits spread muscles out ✓ |
| Frequency | Each major muscle about twice a week (ACE); 3 strength days a week works as well as more (Healthline) | Mostly ✓ |
| Estimated max | Only accurate from sets of 10 reps or fewer (NASM, ACE) | Epley from any set ✗ (review item 14) |
| Power needs a base | Build strength before power; plyometrics need a strength base and a warm-up (Healthline, ACE) | Beginners start with hypertrophy ✓ |
| Warm-up | 5–10 min light cardio plus dynamic moves (Healthline, Nerd Fitness); ACE's version is 2–4 core plus 2–4 bodyweight moves; lighter or bar-only sets before each lift (Nerd Fitness, Healthline) | 5 min counted in time estimate; no warm-up sets (review item 21) |
| Session length | Under an hour (Nerd Fitness) | Set by the user |
| Bodyweight moves | Switch to a harder variation once you pass about 20 reps (Nerd Fitness) | Reps only, gets stuck (review item 11) |
| Compound vs isolation rest | Compounds rest longer than isolation (ACE 2026: 90–180s vs 60–90s for hypertrophy) | Compound longer half, isolation shorter half ✓ |

## Where they disagree

### Endurance

1. **Reps.** ACE and Nerd Fitness 12+; NASM 12–20; Healthline 13–20 in one article, 15+ in another. App: 15–20.
2. **Rest.** ACE 30s or less; Nerd Fitness and Healthline 30–60s (Healthline's rest article says under 2 min, as little as 20s); NASM 0–90s. App: 30–60s.
3. **Sets.** NASM 1–3; Nerd Fitness/NSCA and Healthline 2–3. App: 3, no range.
4. **Load.** ACE and NSCA 67% of max or less; Healthline 40–60%; NASM 50–70%. App: follows from reps.

### Hypertrophy

5. **Rest.** NASM 0–60s; Healthline 30–90s (rest article) or 1–3 min (strength guide); Nerd Fitness 60–90s; ACE 30–90s, or 60–90s isolation and 90–180s compound in its 2026 article. App: 30–90s.
6. **Sets.** NASM and NSCA 3–6; Nerd Fitness 3–5; ACE 3–4; Healthline 3. App: 3–6.

### Strength

7. **Sets.** NASM 4–6; NSCA 2–6; Healthline 4–5 or 3–6. App: 3, no range.
8. **Rest.** ACE and Nerd Fitness 2–5 min; Healthline 3–5 min (strength guide also says 1–3 min saves time). App: 2–5 min.

### Power (the biggest split)

9. **What power training is.**
   - Heavy and few: ACE 1–2 reps at 80–90% or 3–5 reps at 75–85%; NSCA 3–5 sets at 75–85%.
   - Light and fast: Healthline 1–3 sets of 4–10 reps at 30–70%, never to failure; its explosive-workout article uses 3–4 sets of 3–8 (box jumps 5, swings 6–12).
   - Paired: NASM pairs a heavy 1–5 rep set with 8–10 explosive reps.
   - App: 1–10 reps, 2–5 min rest, progresses like the other phases.
   - Rest: 2–5 min (ACE, NSCA), 3+ min (Healthline guide), 2–3 min (Healthline explosive article).

### Phases

10. **Phase length.** NASM changes phase every 4–6 weeks. Healthline says strength shows after 6–15 weeks and muscle after 8–12. Nerd Fitness says about 8 weeks for visible change. App: 13 weeks each.
11. **Deloads.** NASM: a lighter week every 4–6 weeks at about half the volume, or about a third less when progress stalls. Nerd Fitness: on a plateau, spend a week in a different rep range. Healthline: not covered. App: none.

### Tiny steps

12. **How much weight to add.** NASM 2–5% upper body, 5–10% lower body. ACE 5% or less. Healthline 2–10%. Nerd Fitness beginners 2–5 lb upper, 5–10 lb lower (advanced 5–10 and 10–15 lb). App: always 2.5 kg / 5 lb.
13. **When to add weight.**
    - 2-for-2 rule: NASM and Nerd Fitness/NSCA. Add weight when the last set beats the rep target by 2, two sessions in a row.
    - Double progression: ACE and Healthline. Work up to the top of the rep range on every set, then add weight.
    - App: one tiny step each workout, rotating reps → weight → rest, and weight only at the top of the range.
14. **Rest as a step.** Nerd Fitness and Healthline list less rest as a way to progress. Others don't mention it. App: −5s is one of the three steps.
15. **Rest by reps.** Nerd Fitness sets rest by rep count: 1–3 reps 3–5 min, 4–7 reps 2–3 min, 8–12 reps 1–2 min, 13+ until recovered. App: by phase and compound/isolation.

### Beginners

16. **Starting sets.** ACE 1–2 sets, 2 days a week. Healthline 1–2 or 2–3 sets of 8–15. Nerd Fitness 3 × 10. NASM 1–3 sets of 12–20. App: phase defaults.
17. **Beginner rest.** Healthline 60–120s (at least 60s); NASM 0–90s. App: at least 60s.

### Effort

18. **How hard each set is.** NASM hypertrophy goes to failure, with 3–5 reps in reserve for its endurance phase and 1–3 for hypertrophy and strength. ACE 0–3 reps from failure for hypertrophy. Healthline goes to failure for strength and hypertrophy, but never for power. NASM also says beginners can't judge reps in reserve. App: doesn't ask.

### Volume

19. **How many sets.** Nerd Fitness 10–20 total sets per workout. Healthline gives no weekly target (even under 5 sets per exercise per week grows muscle). App: not tracked (review item 19).
